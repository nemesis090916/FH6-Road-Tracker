These are the road segments that players most commonly miss on their way to 100% completion. Each entry includes where it is, why it's easy to skip, and how to find it. Mark them as found to track your own hunt.

💡 Cursor Trick (PC)Hover your mouse cursor slowly along every road on the map. Roads you can fast-travel to are discovered — ones that skip the prompt aren't.
💡 Region FilterPress LB/RB in-game to cycle region views — it draws a border around each area so you can systematically sweep one zone at a time.
💡 OverpassesMulti-level interchanges show as white on the map when only the top layer is driven. The ramp beneath it is a separate road segment.
💡 Dirt Track TailsMany dirt/offroad paths have a short 5–10 m tail at the very end that counts as its own segment. Drive to the absolute end.
💡 Slow DownSome short segments only register if you drive through them slowly or stop briefly. Fast travel then drive out.
💡 The GauntletSeveral players found their final road by completing The Gauntlet race — it routes through some out-of-the-way segments.
